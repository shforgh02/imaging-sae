# Figure plan for the paper (task 5), revised 22 Nov 2026 after Neon's assessment

**In simple words.** Six main figures, in two clean arcs. First the slice-spacing story: what goes wrong (the picture), who was studied (the flow), that the pre-registered tests hold and replicate outside NLST, why it happens, and how often it can happen today. Then, separately, the clinical context: how Sybil, the form and Lung-RADS compare at the same false-alarm level, and why operating points must not be set on a development split's cancers. Every number comes from a committed result file; no figure computes a new result. Neon's assessment: `<scratchpad>/gemini_review/gemini_task5_plan.txt`.
