# Figure plan for the paper (task 5), draft 22 Nov 2026

**In simple words.** Six main figures tell the story in order: what goes wrong (the picture), who was studied (the flow), that the frozen tests hold (the hypotheses), why it happens (the mechanism), what it does to patients (alarms and missed cancers), and how often it can happen today (exposure). Every number comes from a committed result file; no figure computes a new result. Style: plain SVG (no plotting package in the project environment), the validated two-slot palette of `s6_figures.py` (blue `#2a78d6`, orange `#eb6834`) plus ink and grey; one axis per panel; a legend whenever two series appear; intervals always shown.
